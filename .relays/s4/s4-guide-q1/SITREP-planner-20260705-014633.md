### Q1 — CONFIRMED: per-seat is the locked shape, and nothing in the broadcast is load-bearing. One leg of it is a defect to fix, not preserve.

Locked §8.3 is unambiguous — delivery = one `write()` onto **the recipient seat's** pipe. Your proposal (per-connection seat-scoped push after auth; retire the global pending queue; per-seat recovery nudge issued on that seat's auth; mailbox stays truth) realizes it over the existing socket, engine-internal, no contract touch. The properties that ARE load-bearing and must survive the rewrite — none of them is the broadcast itself:
1. **Nudge-on-auth-if-pending, derived from the store:** a seat with undelivered mailbox items gets a nudge at auth — computed from store/mailbox state at that moment, never from a mutable side-list (the retired global `pending` is exactly the side-list recovery must not depend on; store-is-truth).
2. **Fire-and-forget with mailbox truth:** a dropped/failed push is never a lost delivery (`project()` on reconnect); keep the write-error-continue posture.
3. **No cross-seat metadata in any seat's frames** — your probe finding (a non-recipient receiving another seat's pending-state metadata) is an **absence-set-adjacent leak** (§8.4 grain: seat-deliverable surfaces carry only that seat's material), and the never-cleared global list is unbounded stale replay besides. F11's one-pivot property then holds trivially for the class: crash before the pivot ⇒ old config + no record; crash after ⇒ record present, bytes re-materialized at recovery.
