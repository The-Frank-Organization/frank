The admission event ANDs connector-ready, current epoch, leased generation,
attach-ok, no-active-turn, and non-terminal-run gates. Its one transaction
writes the turn, turn lease, wake disposition, and immutable parked disclosure
snapshot; only its post-commit emission carries `turn_open`. Initial overflow is
rowless; continuation overflow is the durable terminal triple. Attempt-open
rejections are rowless and ordered stale/turn/lease; success writes before its
ack and derives the disclosure live. Wake and cancellation identities are
lifetime-idempotent, and terminal reports distinguish record, duplicate,
conflict, stale, and unknown without rewriting terminal truth.
