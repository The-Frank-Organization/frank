# Pre-Foundation Status and Contribution Freeze

**Status:** Private pre-formation project  
**Effective date:** July 30, 2026  
**Current project steward:** Jack Li, in his individual capacity  
**Future intended steward:** An independent nonprofit public-benefit organization that does not yet exist

## Current legal and organizational status

No Frank Foundation, startup company, LLC, or other Frank legal entity currently exists.

This repository's presence in a GitHub organization does not itself establish legal ownership of copyright, trademarks, or other project assets. Jack Li remains the current owner of his original copyrightable contributions, subject to third-party rights, provenance review, and any later signed assignment.

Nothing in this repository should be read as stating that a future Foundation or startup presently owns Frank.

## Contribution freeze

Until an independent Frank steward has been legally formed and has adopted a signed copyright-assignment process:

1. No external code, patch, documentation, protocol text, schema, benchmark, test fixture, prompt, workflow, design asset, image, relay material, or other substantive authored contribution may be merged or incorporated.
2. No pull request, email, message, issue comment, chat transcript, or pasted code excerpt from another person may be treated as project-owned material.
3. Bug reports and high-level ideas may be recorded, but maintainers must independently implement any resulting work without copying the submitter's protected expression.
4. Every human author and co-author of every commit must be identified.
5. A change may be merged only when it was authored solely by Jack Li or a separately signed rights instrument has been reviewed outside GitHub.
6. No agent may infer that an employer, university, contractor, or collaborator has waived ownership.
7. No repository administrator may alter the license, copyright owner, repository visibility, canonical owner, or Git history without a separate written authorization and legal review.

Unsolicited external pull requests or patches must be closed without merge and without copying their implementation into another commit.

## Existing history

The pre-Foundation baseline is identified by a repository-specific tag of the form:

```text
pre-foundation-baseline-2026-07-30
```

The corresponding commit SHA, local Git bundle hash, authorship report, and access inventory are maintained outside the repository in a private evidence archive.

Historical relay or provenance records are evidentiary material. They must not be rewritten, normalized, or selectively removed merely to improve presentation.

## Private access

Repository access is not permission to:

- contribute code or authored material;
- distribute or publish the private repository;
- add collaborators;
- share credentials;
- change repository settings;
- represent that the recipient, a contemplated startup, or a future Foundation owns Frank.

Any person other than Jack Li who receives access should sign a repository-access and non-contribution acknowledgment before receiving continuing access.

## Future process

External contributions may reopen only after:

1. a legal Frank steward exists;
2. the canonical project assets have been transferred through signed instruments;
3. individual and entity contributor copyright-assignment agreements have been adopted;
4. a private assignment registry and required merge check are operational;
5. corporate contributors have supplied employer authorization;
6. the public contribution policy has been reviewed by open-source counsel.

## Scope

This policy applies to every canonical Frank repository, including source, relay/provenance, specification, documentation, benchmark, website, and governance-material repositories.
