## Temporary pre-Foundation contribution freeze

Frank is not presently accepting external code, documentation, specification,
benchmark, design, media, relay, prompt, workflow, or other substantive authored
contributions.

Do not submit implementation material through pull requests, issues, discussions,
email, or chat. Unsolicited pull requests will be closed without merge, and their
protected expression must not be copied into a replacement commit.

Bug reports and high-level functional requests written in your own words may be
recorded. Until a legal Frank steward and signed copyright-assignment process
exist, every merged human-authored change must be authored solely by Jack Li or
covered by a separately reviewed signed rights instrument.

See [`docs/legal/PRE_FOUNDATION_STATUS.md`](docs/legal/PRE_FOUNDATION_STATUS.md).
