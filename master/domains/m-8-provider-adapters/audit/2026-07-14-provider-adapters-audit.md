## 1. Verdict vocabulary

Per the boot: each candidate is **promote** (adopt the behavior/shape as a frank-owned contract element, re-specified fresh), **adapt-with-governance** (the mechanism is right but must be re-homed or re-shaped to respect a governance seam), or **reject** (must not cross into frank; named seam it would violate). pi/opencode are prior art + conformance-fixture sources, NOT the normative interface (kickoff §1) — "promote" therefore always means *frank re-owns the specification*; it never means inheriting the donor's types or its trust assumptions.

## 2. Structural findings that frame every row

- **F-1 (pi has no chokepoint).** pi has no central request envelope and no single send boundary: `onPayload`, `baseUrl`, `apiKey`, `headers` are re-plumbed independently inside each of its 10 wire adapters (e.g. ✓ `api/anthropic-messages.ts:546-556`; `api/openai-completions.ts:185-194`; `api/bedrock-converse-stream.ts:235-238`). Any frank invariant of the form "callers cannot mutate the payload / select the endpoint" is unenforceable *inside* pi's topology — it must live at a layer pi does not have. This is the strongest single argument for the kickoff §1 final-authorization-at-the-governed-send-boundary contract, and it means frank's adapter topology is **fan-in through one governed sender**, not pi's fan-out of self-sufficient adapters.
- **F-2 (pi treats endpoint as auth).** pi's auth layer *defines* auth as `{apiKey?, headers?, baseUrl?}` (✓ `auth/types.ts:8-12`) and a stored credential's `baseUrl` silently **replaces** `model.baseUrl` (✓ `models.ts:248`). Endpoint selection and credential are fused in the donor; frank's split (m-7 owns credential + endpoint binding; m-8 owns translation; catalog carries no secrets) has no donor implementation to port — it is fresh contract surface.
- **F-3 (opencode's catalog is a live, writable, mixed artifact).** opencode's runtime `Model`/`Provider` records mix factual metadata with runtime `options`/`headers`/`key` in one schema (✓ `provider/provider.ts:1029-1054` at HEAD — the schema itself; the merge executes in `resolveSDK:1666-1798` + `getLanguage:1828-1857`), assembled through an eight-stage mutable pipeline where **plugins run first** and may wholesale-replace a provider's model map (`provider.ts:1376-1415`), and the models.dev catalog is a network-fetched, 5-min-TTL, env-overridable source (`core/src/models-dev.ts:154-176`) whose entries **can themselves carry request body/header overrides** (`models-dev.ts:92-109` → `provider.ts:1262-1273`). The *shape* of a models.dev entry is a good import boundary (kickoff §4 approved models.dev-shaped); the *lifecycle* is the exact opposite of frank's pinned/seeded/digest-bound catalog.
- **F-4 (frank's wire layer is fully greenfield).** frank at `s11-close` has zero outbound HTTP anywhere in `internal/`+`cmd/` (no `net/http` client use), no provider/credential/endpoint config member (`internal/config/config.go:186-242` — members are exactly `engine`/`catalog`/`fieldspec`), and touches model identity only as inert payload (`egress/rules.go`, `egress/egress.go` ODB carve-out, `obligation/obligation.go:156`). Nothing to promote from frank *as a wire layer*; everything to respect as governance substrate (§6 below).
