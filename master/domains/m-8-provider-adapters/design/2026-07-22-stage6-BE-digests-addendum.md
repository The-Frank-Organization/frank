## §0 — Three distinct predicates: RECORD presence (P1) · producer ELIGIBILITY (P2a) · evaluator DECIDABILITY (P2b) (R2-F1 + R3-F1)

The digests carry three distinct kinds of fact; r2 fused presence with observability (R2-F1), and r3 then over-equated the producer's write-completion counter with the observer's capture availability (R3-F1). r4 keeps all three apart:

- **P1 — RECORD PRESENCE (a producer fact, keyed on the freeze boundary).** A digest evidence field is **PRESENT** on an m-8 record iff `freeze` completed for that attempt (r12 §2.2 step 2 ran to completion); **ABSENT** otherwise; and where the outcome emits **no m-8 carrier at all**, there is no field to place (N/A — impossible, not absent-optional). Freeze (step 2) runs after translate (step 1) and before authorize (step 3), so the boundary is crisp. Both B and E share P1 (E rides alongside B, amendment §5-E). **Consumers read P1 presence directly off the field — never inferred from `phase` or the `rejected_local` disposition token** (that token spans a freeze-failed no-digest case and a post-freeze has-digest case — §1 row 2).

- **P2a — PRODUCER-SIDE ELIGIBILITY (an m-8-internal cut fact — NOT an E3 input).** Whether a **complete request crossed the §5.1 instrumented boundary** is m-8's own instrumentation — `request_write_completed` (r12 §5.1:195 + fixture 4:217). `request_write_completed=0` (fresh-dial, nothing-written, partial-write) ⇒ **no complete request was ever written**, a producer-side **sufficient reason** that the wire comparison cannot be decided (`unknown`). `request_write_completed=1` ⇒ a complete request was written = the wire leg is **ELIGIBLE (potentially observable)** — but **eligibility is not decidability**. This counter is m-8-internal (fixture 4 asserts on it, r12:195); **m-3 does NOT read or trust it as an E3 input, and it is NOT added to any carrier or E3 record.**

- **P2b — EVALUATOR DECIDABILITY (m-3's OWNER fact — the actual applicability rule).** m-3's `provider_request_matches_frozen_core v1` is **decidable** iff the observer's **`evidence_locator` resolves to a complete captured request** containing every §2.3 (B) / §3.2 (E) derivation input (m-3 delta `dc3b6eb3…`:31,33). Then: `pass` = the observer's **independent** derivation byte-equals the carried digest · `fail` = both derived, unequal · **`unknown` = no wire capture (or no carried digest)** (m-3 delta:33). A completed local write (P2a=1) does **not** prove the independent recorder retained a complete, resolvable capture — capture loss/incompleteness/unavailability is reachable even at `{1,1,1,1}` ⇒ `unknown`. **Equality is NEVER manufactured from m-8's own carried digest** (F84 — a fresh derivation from captured bytes, never a byte-copy of the carried comparand); and an `unknown` leg never passes (m-3 §-common rule).

P1, P2a, P2b are distinct: an attempt can have a PRESENT record (P1, freeze ran) yet an ineligible wire leg (P2a=0 — deny, pre-transport cancel, the fresh-dial/nothing-written cuts) OR an eligible-but-undecidable one (P2a=1 but P2b `unknown` — a complete write whose capture the observer lacks). m-8 owns P1 (the record) + P2a (the producer cut fact); **m-3 owns P2b (E3 decidability).**

### 1.2 The reject-reply discriminator — the decoded 2a/2b field (R2, m-3's producer fact)
r12's `reject_reason` enum (r12:76 — `{malformed_request, lane_capability_mismatch, replay_scope_violation, internal_integrity_fault}`, frozen) separates row 1 from row 2, but **both row 2a AND row 2b carry `reject_reason=internal_integrity_fault`** (r12:85 — duplicate-header freeze refusal and post-authorize send-mismatch refusal share the token). So the reason token does NOT separate 2a (no B) from 2b (B present), and r5 stated the split only in prose. This delta publishes the **decoded discriminator field** so a consumer keeps the two apart **without consulting digest presence** (that would be circular — a producer that wrongly dropped B on a real 2b, or spuriously attached B on a 2a, must be *catchable*, so the classifier cannot decide the case by the very field it validates).

**The field** — `m8.dataP_reply.v2` carries, on **every reject reply** (rows 1/2a/2b), a required member:
```
refusal_stage ∈ { "pre_freeze", "post_freeze" }
```
a positive producer assertion of **where in `freeze→authorize→attach→send` (r12 §2.2) the refusal occurred relative to a COMPLETED freeze** — an independently-meaningful pipeline fact, not a restatement of digest presence:
- **`pre_freeze`** — the refusal happened at or before freeze, so **no `frozen_core` was ever established**: the row-1 translate rejects (`malformed_request`/`lane_capability_mismatch`/`replay_scope_violation`) and the **row-2a** duplicate-frozen-header refusal (freeze interrupted, r12:122).
- **`post_freeze`** — the refusal happened after a completed freeze (a `frozen_core` exists): the **row-2b** send-digest-mismatch refusal (r12:144).

**The total decode (reason × stage):**

| `reject_reason` | `refusal_stage` | row | B/E on the reply |
|---|---|---|---|
| `malformed_request` · `lane_capability_mismatch` · `replay_scope_violation` | `pre_freeze` | 1 | **absent** |
| `internal_integrity_fault` | `pre_freeze` | **2a** | **absent** |
| `internal_integrity_fault` | `post_freeze` | **2b** | **present** (B = the step-2 authorized digest; E = its lowered-tools digest) |

**The checkable invariant (the honesty gain):** on any `m8.dataP_reply.v2` reject reply, **`refusal_stage = post_freeze ⟺ (B and E members present)`**. Because `refusal_stage` is emitted independently of the digest members, a mismatch (post_freeze without B, or pre_freeze with B) is a **detectable producer fault** — m-3 keys the 2a/2b cut off `refusal_stage`, then *validates* B-presence against it (never the reverse). This is the F84 discipline applied to presence: an independent ground-truth fact, not a self-certifying field. `refusal_stage` is a coarse pipeline-position enum — payload-free, no secret, no request content (F67 intact).

**This is a discriminator, not a claim of unreachability:** both stages are reachable and emitted; the field never asserts row 2a "cannot happen." `egress_denied` (row 4, always post-freeze + B) and the epoch replies (row 3, always pre-pipeline + no B) are **distinct reply kinds**, unambiguous by kind — they need no `refusal_stage` (the field is on the reject-reply kind only).

**Mirror on `m8.attempt_result.v2` (CTRL-C → m-10):** the identical 2a/2b ambiguity exists on the attempt result (both carry disposition `rejected_local(internal_integrity_fault)`, r12:85), and B rides there too — so the same `refusal_stage` member rides the **reject dispositions** of `m8.attempt_result.v2`, with the same invariant, so **m-10's row B-presence is equally validatable, not self-certifying**.
