## §Q1 — Broker-survives-app-main-crash: the survival + recovery contract (pinned; mostly a consolidation of r11 §2.2/§2.7/§2.10 with TWO new pins)

**The contract, stated once:** the broker OUTLIVES an app-main crash, the seat channel STAYS BOUND through the outage, and recovery never copies credential bytes and never breaks genesis — because recovery re-establishes the CONTROL session, not the seat channel.

1. **Survival is a supervision property, now PINNED as a named delta (new — CI-4):** m-10 spawns the broker OUTSIDE the app-main's death set — its own process group/session, **no parent-death kill of any form** (no `PDEATHSIG`, no kill-on-parent-exit supervision rule, not a member of any process group the app-main terminates at exit/crash). r11 §2.2 asserted "no parent-death kill is configured"; this delta makes it an **m-10-confirmable interface property** (their spawn discipline), carried as **CI-4** beside CI-1..3 in the §4 consumer table. Without this pin the whole §Q1 contract silently degrades to luck.
2. **The seat channel through the outage (r11 §2.7 app-main-crash row, corrected per R2-F1):** the channel stays bound (no writer exists to advance the epoch during the outage); the fence fails closed at the §2.4 suspended floor the instant the control session drops (reject `broker:suspended`, suppress forwards). **Child fate:** app-main EOF makes the worker and connector fail closed and EXIT (frozen m-10 §B.3) — so the admitting worker connection is ordinarily GONE; delivery of an old in-flight response is CONDITIONAL on the admitting connection still being alive (§Q3.4), never universal (rev2's "complete and deliver normally" is RETRACTED as a universal claim). **The Q3 delta below REMOVES the crossing-row clause of that row**; the withheld-payload rule survives only for the remaining response-coupled event class.
3. **Recovery = control adoption + the LIFECYCLE-STATE-DEPENDENT generation path, distinguished (R2-F1):**
   **(a) Broker-CONTROL adoption** — the replacement app-main adopts the surviving broker by the r11 §2.10 machinery verbatim (`broker-control.lock` fcntl → durable `control_generation` advance → connect → peer/lock probe + token + strictly-greater generation → the step-5 snapshot branch). Adoption itself NEVER mints an epoch, moves no credential material, and touches neither the seat channel nor the binding (the token is a control capability; S-B never moves).
   **(b) Generation recovery** — what happens to the EPOCH is m-10's lifecycle-state matrix, CONSUMED here, never overridden: in the ordinary case — a durable `LEASED` generation with no committed retirement — m-10's ONE keyed retirement transaction runs exactly once (parking consumed/no-outcome F59 rows state-sensitively per §Q3.4) and MINTS E+1; the §Q3 broker transition then installs E+1 BEFORE successor assign/attach (m-10 §B.4 retirement ⇒ new epoch; the old generation's E authority is REVOKED by retirement, never restored). **No-mint recovery exists ONLY in m-10's already-current branches** (`RETIRED_PENDING_REAP`, pre-lease wash-out, initial E=1) exactly as their matrix defines — it is a property of those branches, never of app-main recovery in general. "No channel rebind / no re-auth / no credential movement" holds INDEPENDENTLY of epoch movement in every branch.
4. **The only path that re-establishes the seat channel is broker death itself** (r11 §2.7 broker-crash row, unchanged): the replacement broker re-reads the §2.12 operator-authored credential sink and re-authenticates — the sink file persists across every process death, so there is **no genesis break** (no re-mint is needed; the same credential re-binds per s6 §F) and **no credential-bytes copy** (the sink is the ONE resolution path; nothing is inherited, transferred, or cached outside broker memory).
5. **The suspended deadline stays supervision policy** (r11 §2.10: `CONTROL_REATTACH_DEADLINE`; a suspended broker never self-authorizes; killing/respawning it is m-10's call). Unchanged.

## §Q2 — Adoption of the logical m-9 seat channel by a fresh worker generation (the linearization, pinned)

2. **The linearization is TOTAL BY CONSTRUCTION — one serialization point + assign-after-install:** worker replacement is exactly one epoch transition (E → E+1) at the broker's single serialization point (r11 §2.5, as simplified by §Q3). The order every observer sees:

## §Q3 — Cross-epoch completion: the DETERMINATION

**RECOMMENDATION: the SIMPLER RULE SET — an epoch boundary is a clean cut. Cross-epoch completion is NOT retained. Consequently NO H-24 is required.**

### Q3.1 The rule set (exact, replacing r11's complete-and-deliver)

- **kill-all-children:** worker teardown at replacement stays m-10/F66 policy (unchanged; not broker behavior).
- **rebind:** the successor generation attaches fresh (§Q2; the r11 attach machinery unchanged).
- **no-op-crosses-epoch:** NO operation admitted under E completes, delivers, or forwards after E+1 installs. A response arriving from the conductor after INSTALL for a cut operation is **DISCARDED** (never delivered, never buffered for the successor — delivering it would be exactly the cross-epoch completion this determination rejects). No push crosses (forward-time fence, r11 §2.4 unchanged).
- **bounded-drain:** at PROPOSED the broker stops all E admission/send/forward immediately (R5-F1 verbatim) and opens a drain window of compiled-constant length (`EPOCH_DRAIN_DEADLINE`, one conductor-round-trip class) for already-SENT operations; completions arriving in-window deliver normally under E (pre-install, ordinary).
- **unresolved→UNKNOWN:** at drain expiry every still-unresolved operation is terminated broker-side, typed by verb: `relay.submit` ⇒ **`broker:unknown-outcome`** (the first attempt may have committed — the honest class, already in the r11 vocabulary); `relay.project`/`relay.read`/`Describe` ⇒ **`broker:stale-epoch`** (read-only or disposition-stable — re-invoke under the successor; the r11 §1.5.4 read/quarantine analysis carries: the repair proceeds server-side regardless). Delivery of the typed response is CONDITIONAL on a live admitting connection (§Q3.4); for the three F59 relay tool calls the durable disposition rides m-10's outcome map either way (`Describe` per its separate posture, §Q3.4). Then INSTALL proceeds atomically.
- **The cut is BROKER-LOCAL and TOTAL across control loss (R2-F2):** the drain deadline runs from PREPARING entry INDEPENDENTLY of controller availability — control loss neither pauses nor restarts it. At expiry the broker IRREVOCABLY cuts every unresolved old-E operation; EVERY later response for a cut operation is discarded, whether control is present or not. **INSTALL completes at the local expiry too** (E+1 was durably published BEFORE the proposal — the proposal is authority, R5-F1; nothing further is owed): while control remains lost, the installed E+1 state is RETAINED but NON-AUTHORIZING under the existing §2.4/attach discipline (operations reject `broker:suspended`; no capability mints) until verified control returns — E is never revived and no second drain ever runs for the cut identities. **Re-proposal after control return is IDEMPOTENT over the cut state:** an equal proposal acks; it cannot create a second cut, a second disposition, or a new drain for already-cut identities; only a genuinely NEWER epoch starts a new transition over the successor-epoch in-flights.
- **rediscovery+dedup:** truth recovery is the landed machinery — the conductor record is truth; a re-submitted duplicate replays by intake content-hash instead of re-executing (r11 FX-TB-11, proven not assumed); `project`/`read`/`Describe` through the successor capability recover every outcome. No auto-resend anywhere: the broker never re-issues a cut submit; re-submission is the CALLER's informed act after rediscovery.

And in the app-main-down window, r11 §2.11 already WITHHELD crossing payloads (`broker:record-unavailable`). Retention preserved a delivery path whose realizable value is a narrow race — at the price of the heaviest machinery in the contract.
2. **One outcome carrier, not two — the item-D coherence argument (corrected per R1-F1).** The stage-6 amendment's durable-resume subsystem already gives every unprovable in-flight an honest home: `uncertain`, park-not-replay, no fabricated settled effect, no auto-resend. **The clean cut CONSUMES that ratified machinery rather than adding to it:** a relay tool call IS a settlement member — the frozen m-9 contract routes all eight tools (relay included) through the uniform F59 authorize/consume/record path with NO relay carve-out, and the canonical app-side outcome record is m-10's `tool_calls` row — so a cut relay call lands in the EXISTING state-sensitive map (§Q3.4) and the EXISTING D2 `uncertain` class. (rev1's contrary claim conflated two records with different owners: the item-D table excludes **conductor relay records** from the m-9 CONTENT log — that exclusion says nothing about the m-10 OUTCOME rows, which stay in settlement. CONCEDED.) Retaining cross-epoch completion, by contrast, would add broker-delivered late completions as a SECOND outcome carrier competing with m-10's canonical rows — the two-sided seam that would need its own join model and H-24. The simpler rule leaves ONE outcome carrier (m-10's rows) and ONE effect truth (the conductor record), with the manifest untouched: no new class, nothing fabricated.
3. **The eliminated machinery is exactly the part that was at the edge of hand-verifiability.** r11's transition ledger, crossing rows, `completed-before-install`, and the five-row recovery matrix took reviews r3–r10 to stabilize, and the dispatch's own H-24 conditional prices retained completion at a bounded formal model. Cut the crossings and the properties H-24 would prove become constructional: **no double-adopt** (one serialization point + assign-after-install — §Q2.2's total order); **no lost completion** (nothing survives to lose: in-window completions are ordinary same-epoch deliveries; cut operations are typed UNKNOWN, and the truth is the conductor record, which cannot be lost by any broker/app event); **no duplicated completion** (single connection-scoped delivery path, discard-after-install, content-hash dedup on re-submit); **no credential-bytes escape** (untouched by this determination — F67/§2.12 stand). 4. **What F64 required is still met, made exact (corrected per R3-F3 — no universal "recorded" claim).** The F64 **FENCE** is unweakened for ALL FOUR broker operations (every verb + `Describe` + every push gates per-operation; connect-time auth stays insufficient).
