  **Why rows 3, 4, 6 and 10 exist (r9-F1):** the actor changes at each of them, and an unrecorded actor change is an unrecorded handoff. A proposal is consumed by **master**, which materializes and recomputes before the implementer can verify anything; equality and content are **two distinct duties at two distinct points**, so one generic verdict cannot prove which the return descends from; and a disposition that nothing parents to lets the flow rejoin while skipping the escalation outcome. **Operator prompting and filesystem visibility are never the handoff** — only a relay is.

  **Rework is a new generation, not a reuse (r10-F1).** A relay *kind* may instantiate more than once; every **instance** must still be mechanically unique, so the content-review pair is parameterized by **review generation `<r>`** exactly as proposals are parameterized by `<n>`.   **Rework edges (explicit, so no rework silently re-parents):** an equality confirmation that **fails** is the parent of the corrective `…-l4-propose-<n'>`; a content verdict of **revise** is the parent of the first corrective `…-l4-propose-<n'>`. Rework never rejoins by pointing back at a superseded predecessor.
