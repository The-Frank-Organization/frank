## Item 5 — the typed predicate: `successor_admitted_at_current_epoch_under_valid_lease` v1
In my frozen common shape (r24 §2:102) `{predicate_id, version, required_inputs[], observed_facts[], evidence_locator, verdict ∈ pass|fail|unknown, observer_id, …}`. The predicate **WRAPS m-10's frozen gate as observed evidence** — it does not redefine "admitted at current epoch under valid lease" (that is rev16 §4:55 atomic lease-at-current-epoch admission + §6:130's exactly-once tuple-keyed assign gate, m-10's). It renders the E3 verdict over BOTH arms:
- **required_inputs / observed_facts:** the m-10 admission/assign-gate outcome record for the **positive-control** successor (current epoch, valid lease) · the same for the **induced-fault** successor (stale/wrong epoch-or-lease, m-10's item 1) · the zero-successor-work evidence for the rejected arm.
- **verdict machine** (all conditions must hold for `pass`; first failing condition ⇒ `fail`; unresolved evidence ⇒ `unknown`):
  1. evidence for either arm does not resolve / incomplete capture ⇒ **`unknown`** (r24 §2 rule; never a false `pass`).
  2. **positive arm:** the legitimate successor **IS admitted** by the gate **and proceeds** (commits/does its turn work) ⇒ required for `pass`.
  3. **negative arm:** the stale/wrong-epoch-or-lease successor is **refused fail-closed** (m-10's exact durable/wire outcome, item 3) **AND performs zero successor work** — no provider send, no tool call, no durable effect (m-10's item 4) ⇒ required for `pass`.
  4. `pass` **iff both arms hold**; **`fail`** if a stale/wrong successor is admitted OR performs any work, OR the legitimate successor is refused.
- **Refusal-alone never passes** (the `xit-dur-4` lesson master cited): a worker that refuses *everything*, or that exposes a runnable admit transition it never executes, must NOT pass — which is why the **positive control is a `pass` precondition, not decoration**. Discrimination, not mere refusal, is the property.
- The predicate is a **discrimination** term: it proves the gate admits the legitimate and rejects the illegitimate, distinguishing successor-*legitimacy* from the prefix-*identity* the Route-2 leg proves (a stale successor could reproduce the prefix yet must still be refused — the exact false-PASS axis I isolated in 2c).
