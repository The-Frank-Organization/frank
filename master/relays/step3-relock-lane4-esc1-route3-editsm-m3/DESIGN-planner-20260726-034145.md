## 1 — What is observable about an edited prefix (and the hard limit)
From the evidence ladder's vantage the ladder CAN observe **divergence**: the durable content no longer matches the frozen evidence it holds — a stored receipt/marker identity (m-10's settlement snapshot, prior receipt identity) that no longer equals the on-disk bytes. So **an edit is DETECTABLE as "these bytes ≠ the evidence I have."**

The hard limit, stated because it governs the whole design: **my evaluator CANNOT observe *why* the bytes diverge.** At the bytes, a sanctioned hand-repair, a `bivpak` path-rewrite, and an accidental corruption are **indistinguishable** — all three are "content that differs from the frozen capture." My vantage yields divergence, not legitimacy. Therefore **classification (sanctioned vs corrupt vs edited-untrusted) MUST be supplied by an AUTHENTICATED edit-provenance record that the ladder can OBSERVE** — authored by the edit path (m-9/m-10 mechanism, m-1 provenance). It must **not** be inferred by my evaluator from the bytes looking plausible: inferring legitimacy from self-consistency is the exact over-claim my domain forbids, and — importantly — the **confusion-not-malice model does not rescue it**, because an *accidental* edit that happens to produce plausible bytes is still not the original capture. No authenticated record ⇒ untrusted by default.

## 2 — What is assertable: the trust ruling (feeds m-9+m-10 item 3, total over content kinds)
The evidence ladder's basis for trusting provider/tool content is **observe-as-send / observe-as-received through the governed path**, i.e. the frozen **evidence-AND-current-presence** invariant: the content was frozen at capture AND is currently present. An edited byte satisfies *current presence* but **breaks *frozen evidence*** — the frozen evidence is the original capture; the edit is not what was observed. Consequently:
- **Edited provider/tool content is at most `untrusted-but-model-visible`, and never `trusted`.** It may be shown to the model as continuation context (Decision 5 requires resume proceed — repairability), but it **cannot carry the provider/tool-truth label**. Promoting an edited byte to "the provider's/tool's actual output" is precisely the fabrication class m-9's own §9 survey faults deepagents for (synthesising a tool result for a call it cannot account for).
- **Totality, no default-to-trusted:** over every content kind (provider output, tool result, model turn), an edited region is `untrusted`/`degraded` unless an authenticated edit record raises it — and even a sanctioned edit does **not** restore the *provider/tool-truth* rung (a deliberate tool edit is honest history, but it is the editor's content, not the provider's capture). This is the VP constraint made operational: **an edited byte must not silently inherit the trust status of the original.**

Where an edit is authenticated and sanctioned, the honest label is "edited, by <provenance>, superseding the original capture" — history, surfaced, not truth-inherited. That labelling belongs to m-9+m-10's items 2/6; my consequence is only the *rung*: it lands strictly below original-capture provider/tool truth.
