To keep **anti-stale-approval** at the window edge, that re-mint must **supersede the prior cycle's outstanding tokens** — i.e. an old-window token can never wake a re-summoned gate:
- **Trigger = m-6-owned** (the §4 resummon cadence fires the re-mint request).
