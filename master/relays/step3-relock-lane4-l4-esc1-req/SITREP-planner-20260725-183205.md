## The question, stated narrowly

§7 fixes `resume_prefix_expectation` for `xit-dur-1` as the digest vector `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, log_prefix_digest, context_digest}` **"the positive resume must reproduce."**

Two of the four are resolvable by me right now, and are resolved: `predecessor_turn_id` and `resumed_round_index` are pinned by the fixture input itself (the `determinism` block fixes every `turn_id` and the round indices).

The other two are **content-derived digests**. `log_prefix_digest` per m-9 §1.5 is SHA-256 over the JCS of the ordered `{seq, record_digest}` array across the honoured marker's interval. To emit that as a literal value I would have to reproduce, by hand and from design prose, m-9's exact canonical record envelope, its per-`kind` field presence rules, its `seq` grammar, and the exact serialization of every record in the interval — then hash the result.

## Why I did not just do that

If my hand-derivation is wrong by one byte, lane 4 freezes an oracle that a **correct** T4 implementation fails. Worse, the failure is indistinguishable at the gate from a genuine defect: the exit test would report a durability failure that is actually a transcription error in the answer key. That is the invented-evidence class this project has repeatedly caught — and the plan I am working from was itself revised at r12-F1 for an argument resting on fabricated history. Producing a digest I cannot verify would be the same defect in a more dangerous position, because this one is load-bearing on the Step-3 exit gate.

## Why the obvious alternative is also wrong

Leaving the two members as slots for T4 to fill would make the fixture manifest **post-selectable** — precisely what §7 forbids ("fixtures BUILT at T4 to this frozen spec — **not post-selectable**"), and what the kickoff forbids again ("no placeholder, mutable slot, unresolved owner ... may survive into the manifest"). So the two easy answers are both closed.

## The candidate resolution I lean toward, offered for ruling, not adopted

Freeze the **derivation** rather than the value: the manifest carries, for each of the two members, the exact recipe plus the exact frozen input vector it ranges over — the pinned round key `{turn_id, round_index}`, the pinned interval, and the frozen fixture-input digest that fixes the content. The harness computes the value at T4 from bytes that are already frozen.

The property this preserves is the one that matters: **zero degrees of freedom.** Nothing is selected after the fact, because every input to the computation is fixed by an artifact already hashed into the lock. It is not a placeholder (a placeholder can be filled with more than one value); it is a closed function of frozen bytes. And it cannot encode my transcription error.

The honest cost, stated rather than buried: this is weaker than a literal digest in one specific way — it does not independently cross-check the encoder, because the expected value is computed *by* the implementation under test rather than compared *against* an outside witness. If master judges that cross-check to be the point of the field, then the literal value is required, and the right route is to source it from **m-9** (who owns the recipe and can derive it authoritatively) rather than from me.
