## Q1 — Does `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, boundary marker_digest}` already prove the exact resumed-context identity? — NO, and I can say why precisely

The three members prove a real and strong property, but a **different** one from what VP r7 required:
- **What they DO prove:** the durable **session-content LOG PREFIX** was reproduced **byte-exact** at the correct round from the correct predecessor turn. Reading (b) of m-9's answer (`…-esc1-m9/…-185400`) is sound — the boundary `round_marker`'s `marker_digest` is **prefix-DETERMINING** (every record chains via `prev_digest`/`record_digest`; a moved/altered prefix fails closed). So prefix-identity is genuine: the durable **state** recovered faithfully.
- **What they do NOT prove — and it is exactly what Q1 names:** the **model-visible continuation input** and the **settlement snapshot**. The model does not read the log prefix; it reads an **assembled `input[]`** that is a *function of* the prefix + assembly logic + manifest/build. Prefix-identity witnesses the **input to assembly**, never the assembled result.
- **The nearest existing witness is insufficient, verified at the bytes:** `logical_surface_digest` (item-E, m-9 §6, the one I bound at m-3 r24) binds the five-member **pre-lowering surface** `{instructions, logical_tool_schemas[], tool_descriptions[], compaction_template, policy_messages}` and **DELIBERATELY EXCLUDES the assembled `input[]` items** — m-9 §6:389 verbatim: *"the object binds the pre-lowering surface … **never the assembled `input[]` items**."* So even `logical_surface_digest` does not witness the model-visible continuation content; it witnesses the surface around it. And the **settlement snapshot** (the m-10 manifest-union state the leg reconciles against) is likewise outside the log-prefix `marker_digest`.

**So: an independent context witness IS required — with ONE exit.** The gap closes *without* a new witness **iff** m-9 can **freeze and prove, as a gate-checkable lock property, that the assembled model-visible continuation input is a deterministic, closed function of the frozen-identified log prefix + frozen manifest/build, with no un-witnessed degrees of freedom.** If that determinism holds and is *proven* (not assumed), then prefix-identity transitively proves context-identity and `context_digest` is derivable/redundant. **That determinism is an m-9 assembly-domain claim m-3 cannot certify, it is not currently stated, and the Gate-2 reassembly note (assembled `input[]` explicitly outside the frozen surface) is evidence against assuming it.** Absent that proof, the witness is required. A **name** for the witness is what rev8 supplied and it failed; if authored, it needs a **producer/consumer split + an observer-executable recipe** (computable and comparable by a fixture observer) — and that is amendment-shaped (§7 field), Master+VP+operator.

## Q2 — the evidence-sufficiency question (mine): if `context_digest` were struck, what does the Durability POSITIVE leg still prove?

**Still proves:** *a resume reproduced the byte-exact durable log prefix (prefix-determining) at the same round from the same predecessor turn, and reconciled positively against the settlement manifest union.* Real, and worth having — it certifies durable-state recovery.

**Stops proving — and this is the load-bearing loss:** *that the model was presented the same assembled continuation context on resume.* The leg would then have **no mechanical means to distinguish a true resume-from-the-exact-prefix from a re-derivation that reproduces a matching-looking prefix while assembling a DIFFERENT model-visible `input[]`** — a re-log that reconstructs identical records, a compaction that fires differently, any assembly DOF not captured by the prefix. Such a resume reproduces `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, marker_digest}` (and even `logical_surface_digest`, since that excludes `input[]`) yet presents the model a different context — **and the gate scores it PASS.**

That is precisely the case the ratified §7 row says must FAIL: **"A degraded re-derivation never satisfies `xit-dur-1`."** Without a context witness, the leg **cannot enforce its own headline requirement** — "resume from the **exact last valid prefix**" in the sense of what the model continues from. So striking `context_digest` does not remove a belt-and-suspenders check; **it removes the only witness that separates "resumed from the exact prefix" from "re-derived a matching prefix," which is the distinction the Durability positive leg exists to make.**

**On the asymmetry you named — I confirm it from the evidence side.** Authoring a witness we did not strictly need costs work. Striking a witness we did need costs a **false PASS on the Step-3 exit gate**, discovered only when a real resume silently diverges — and it would be *invisible* to the gate by construction, because the gate would have no term that could catch it. For an exit gate, "cannot rule out a false PASS on its own headline property" is disqualifying regardless of how likely the divergence is in any one build; the gate's job is to *prove* the property, not to hope assembly happened to be deterministic. **`context_digest` (or an equivalent context witness) is load-bearing, not vestigial. My earlier lean-adjacent instinct to treat it as strikeable is wrong, and I withdraw any such reading.**

**The clean decision fork this produces (for Master+VP+operator, not me to resolve):** either **(i)** m-9 establishes + freezes assembly-determinism-over-the-frozen-prefix as a gate-checkable lock property (⇒ context derivable, `context_digest` redundant, and the §7 field can be ratified as that derivation), **or (ii)** an independent context witness recipe is authored — a producer/consumer split with an observer-executable digest over the model-visible continuation `input[]` + the settlement snapshot (amendment-shaped). The current state — a ratified field with **no recipe and no determinism proof** — satisfies **neither**, which is exactly the unfulfilled requirement the VP recovered.
