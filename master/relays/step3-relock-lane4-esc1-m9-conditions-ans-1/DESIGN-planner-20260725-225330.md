## Proposal 1 — CONFIRM, strictly stronger; here is the authorable-vs-derived split
Pinning the **authored canonical record CONTENTS** and letting the harness derive every fingerprint satisfies condition (ii)'s intent and is strictly stronger: the transcription-error class disappears, condition (iii) holds **by construction** (the harness derives `expected`; the build never can), and comparison at any depth (full vector or boundary marker) becomes possible from one frozen material. I accept it as the replacement for "pin the ordered `{seq, record_digest}` vector."

**The clean split (this is the thing you asked me to verify at my bytes):**
- **HARNESS-DERIVED — the five log-structure digests, never hand-authored:** `record_digest` (§1.3: SHA-256 over the record's canonical bytes with `record_digest` self-excluded), `prev_digest` (§1.3: the prior record's `record_digest`, or the `segment_open` seed SHA-256(JCS{run_id, run_manifest_digest, segment_id})), `marker_digest` (§1.5: over the ordered `{seq, record_digest}` of `[first_seq,last_seq]`), `boundary_digest` (§1.7: = `boundary_record`'s `record_digest`), `prior_boundary_digest` (§1.7: = the predecessor segment's `boundary_digest`). The harness computes these **iteratively from the authored contents** — for each record set `prev_digest` from the chain, then `record_digest` over the canonical bytes; then the markers/boundaries. Nothing about them is authored.
- **AUTHORABLE-BY-CONSTRUCTION — everything else:** the envelope `{seq, kind, segment_id, generation_id, turn_id?, round_index?, ts_monotonic}`; the `round_marker` `{round_index, first_seq, last_seq}`; the `segment_seal` `{sealed_through_seq, next_segment_id?}`; the `segment_open` `{run_id, run_manifest_digest, segment_id, generation_id, rotation_index, turn_epoch, prior_segment_id?}`; and all payloads (`input_item`, `tool_call` incl. its **`canonical_args_digest` as authored-opaque content**, `tool_result`, `provider_output`, `compaction_event`, `turn_scope`, `objective_ref`, `workspace_snapshot`).

**Answering your sink question directly — is any member engine-assigned rather than authorable? NO, with one member named to watch:**
- `seq`, `generation_id`, `turn_epoch`, `run_manifest_digest`, and **`ts_monotonic`** are **assigned at RUNTIME** (by the engine / m-10 `assign`), but in a **frozen fixture they are authored as fixed values** — and, decisively, they belong to the **durable prefix that resume READS, never re-stamps**, so the `actual` side reproduces them verbatim. `ts_monotonic` is the one to name explicitly (a monotonic clock reading at runtime): it is safe here **only because the prefix-up-to-boundary is durable and replayed, never re-stamped on resume** — if any future durability scenario had the build re-author a prefix record, that record's `ts_monotonic` would be non-deterministic and would have to be harness-supplied under a stated rule. For `xit-dur-1` (read the durable prefix, resume past the boundary), no prefix member is re-stamped, so every authored scalar is reproduced.
- `canonical_args_digest` is a **payload** digest (over tool args), not a log-structure digest; the fixture authors it as opaque content and the harness includes it verbatim in `record_digest` — it does not need harness re-derivation for the log-structure digests, and it is a durable-replayed field, so it is reproduced.

So: **no member is engine-assigned-and-non-authorable-and-non-derivable.** The digests are harness-derived; the runtime scalars are authored-fixed and replayed. P1 stands. CONFIRM.

## Proposal 2 — CONFIRM the basis, with two refinements
**(a) Is the indistinguishability claim correct at one checkpoint? YES — and the precise mechanism is sharper than "same value only at one checkpoint."**
On the **positive resume path** (which is what `xit-dur-1` tests), a build implementing reading (a) and one implementing (b) compute the **SAME `log_prefix_digest` value and the SAME pass verdict — at ANY checkpoint count** — because both reference the **boundary(=resumed)-round's `marker_digest`**, and a correct positive resume reproduces it identically. So the positive test **alone never distinguishes (a) from (b)**, regardless of round count. What distinguishes a **chain-transitive (b)** build from a **non-transitive (a)** build (one that digests only the resumed round's content and does not chain `prev_digest`) is a **NEGATIVE leg that mutates history BEHIND the boundary and asserts `log_prefix_digest` DIVERGES**. At exactly one durable `round_marker` there is essentially no round-level history behind the boundary (only `segment_open` + the turn-setup records), so the **round-to-round chaining that reading (b) rests on is never exercised** — ratifying (b) on a one-checkpoint fixture ratifies an unexercised claim. That is the same designed-but-unexercised shape m-10/m-3 surfaced for the fencing gate. **So your basis is correct; the refinement is that ≥2 rounds are needed to give a NEGATIVE leg round-level history to mutate — the positive `xit-dur-1` never distinguishes the readings by itself.**

**(b) Are two checkpoints sufficient, or is a cross-segment case also needed? A CROSS-SEGMENT CASE IS ALSO NEEDED.**
The chain has **two distinct link types**, and reading (b)'s transitive claim rests on **both**:
- **within-segment** `prev_digest` chaining (§1.3 — each `record_digest` covers its `prev_digest` = the prior record's `record_digest`);
- **cross-segment** `prior_boundary_digest` chaining (§1.7 — a `segment_open` carries `prior_boundary_digest` = the predecessor segment's `boundary_digest`, re-derived and compared by the boundary equation on recovery).
Two `round_marker`s **in the same segment** exercise only the within-segment link. A resume whose valid prefix spans a rotation relies on the cross-segment link, which two same-segment rounds never touch. **So two same-segment checkpoints are NOT sufficient to test the chaining end-to-end.**

**Recommended form of the strengthening (for your packet; I do not ratify a §7 change):** the durability scenario scripts
1. **≥2 `fsync`-durable `round_marker`s**, the resume boundary at the later one — real round-level history behind the boundary;
2. **the valid prefix spans ≥2 SEGMENTS** (a rotation), the boundary in the later segment — so `prior_boundary_digest` (§1.7) is on the recovery path; and
3. **NEGATIVE legs** that each assert `log_prefix_digest` diverges (fail-closed): (i) mutate a record in an **earlier same-segment round** (exercises `prev_digest`); (ii) mutate a record in the **prior segment** (exercises `prior_boundary_digest`). **These negative legs are what actually distinguish a chaining (b) build from a non-chaining (a) build** — the positive resume establishes reproduction, the negatives establish that the ratified reading is under test.

Whether legs (3) live in `xit-dur-1` itself or a companion durability fixture is a fixture-composition call I leave to lane 4 + you; the requirement is that **some exit fixture exercises both chain link types with a mutation leg**, else reading (b) is ratified-but-unexercised.
