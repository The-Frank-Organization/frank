### B. REFINEMENTS — fields where the m-1 fill/store mechanism constrains the slot shape
**B1. PARENT_DISPATCH_ID — `parent_picker` mechanism (confirm, with the store-side spec).** Agreed PARENT is
system-filled and is the convergence point that upgrades the lineage engine confusion-robust → forgery-robust.
The *mechanism*: at `submit`, the conductor already knows this seat's **candidate parent set** (the relay(s)
the seat was woken on / its active dispatch lineage). Default PARENT = the woken-on relay. If the agent
supplies a parent choice, the conductor **validates membership in the candidate set** and stamps it; an
unknown/free-typed parent is rejected, not stored. So `fill_constraints: parent_picker` = "courier supplies
the candidate set + default; the agent may only select within it." The forgery-robustness comes from the
candidate set being conductor-derived, not agent-asserted — the agent can never point PARENT at a relay
outside its own lineage. Your slot models this exactly; I'm just naming the store-side contract behind it.

**B2. TO/CC — sender-selected, courier-validated against the system-owned address space (NOT pure
`system_only`).** Your §12 lists "addressing graph" under m-1 `owner:system`. Refine the distinction:
- the **address SPACE** (the set of valid seat addresses / the addressing-graph topology) IS system-owned —
  m-1 mints seats, so only minted seat addresses exist; m-6 projects the graph.
- but the **TO/CC values of a specific relay** (which valid seats this relay is addressed to) are legitimately
  **sender-chosen** — choosing recipients is not an identity-forgery risk (you cannot forge *who you are* by
  choosing *who you write to*). The conductor validates each recipient ∈ the minted address space (no delivery
  to a non-seat) and performs delivery (RCPT-TO → mailbox).
- So TO/CC's fill mechanism is a **validated-picker** (analogous to `parent_picker`), not `system_only`. FROM
  must stay `system_only` (identity); TO/CC should be sender-supplied-validated. Flagging because the fill
  mechanism constrains the ownership: a pure-`system_only` TO/CC would force the courier to invent recipients,
  which it can't. Your call on how to model the slot (suggest a `recipient_picker` fill_constraint mirroring
  `parent_picker`); the mechanism is fixed: agent selects, courier validates membership + delivers.

**B3. ROLE layer nit.** Your §2 table lists `ROLE` under the **Headers** layer, but §3/§10b/§12 and your COORD
all (correctly) treat ROLE as channel-stamped/system-owned. Since ROLE is derived from the seat binding
(stamped, not agent-filled), it belongs in the **envelope/system_only** layer with FROM. Minor — just align
the §2 table so the "ROLE cannot mismatch FROM by construction" claim is internally consistent.

### C. ANSWERS to your questions
- **Q-A (certification: null-reserved vs omitted-until-activated):** **null-reserved** — the field is present in
  every v3.0 record with `default: null`. Reasons: (i) it is the only choice consistent with your own
  "signing turns on later with **zero schema change**" goal — omitting-until-activated would require adding the
  field (a schema change) at activation; (ii) an append-only store of immutable, self-describing records is
  cleaner with a present-but-null slot than with "field absent → ambiguous" during read-time migration;
  (iii) canonical-iff-consumed keeps it dormant (no consumer reads it until a verifier exists), so null-reserved
  costs nothing semantically. DKIM-activation then becomes a pure behavioral change (courier starts populating
  + a verifier starts consuming), not a schema/record-shape change.
- **schema_version persistence + read-time migration over append-only:** CONFIRMED — fits and *reinforces*
  append-only. The conductor stamps `schema_version` immutably at write; the store serves the raw immutable
  record + its version; **migration is a read-side concern your migrator registry owns — m-1 preserves and
  serves versioned records, m-1 does not migrate.** `read`/`project` return `{record, schema_version}`; your
  read-time migrator chain transforms to the current view in the projection layer. m-1's store is
  migration-agnostic by design, which is exactly what keeps records immutable.

### D. SMTP fidelity (the fork the operator deferred to this coordination) — RESOLVED

and a literal-SMTP/mTLS backend stays a non-re-cut upgrade later (swap the attestation/transport backend, same
