## §3 — SCHEDULER PARK/WAKE + AWAY-MODE BRIDGE (acceptance #4)

**Positioning (R3):** our park/wake is the **Temporal/durable-execution "signal + await-condition + durable timer"** shape — NOT the AG2 blocking-stdin anti-pattern. The decisive property R3 surfaces: **durability is owned by the store, never by a live process** — and **the conductor's sole-writer append-only relay store already *is* that durable checkpointer**. A parked lane consumes nothing; it is woken by a durably-recorded verdict relay. **Polling is a read-only status side-channel only** (export: "polling is fine for *parked* lanes", `:34`); the verdict path is the durable mutating channel.

**Park/wake (local-first — the Step-2 mechanism, default, no external send):**
- **Park:** a gated lane (A-bucket: `HUMAN_GATE=raised` / egress-blocked) parks; the conductor holds the dispatch open.
- **Wake-on-reply:** the operator-FROM verdict relay (§2) is the durable signal that wakes the parked lane. Skeleton = **`WhenAny(verdict-event, resummon-timer)`** (R3/Azure Durable Functions): event → resume + cancel timer; timer → re-summon (NOT auto-resolve, per J1).
- **Resummon cadence:** **notify → wait → re-notify → escalate the SUMMON CHANNEL → bounded-repeat → park-and-stop** (R3/PagerDuty), with two timers (no-response vs answered-but-stalled). **Critical adaptation to J1:** the escalation escalates the *channel* (local inbox → louder → away-bridge), it **never escalates to a verdict** — the conductor never decides a governance gate.

**Away-mode external-inbox bridge — THE one genuinely-new design (SILENT in the export; R2):**
- **Trigger:** operator declares "out" (away-mode = the OOF-detection input, R3/Power-Automate — don't burn escalation levels on a known-away operator). **Opt-in, not default** (default = local in-app inbox; `ARCHITECTURE.md:84-87`).
- **Behavior:** mirror **A-bucket ODBs** to the operator's real inbox (e.g. Gmail); the reply becomes the verdict. **First external send ⇒ egress-gated** by m-3 §7 (`m-3 …design.md:116-122`), fail-closed.
- **DQ-1 RESOLVED + the one cross-domain COORD — inbound verdict trust.** The c1 identity model makes *outbound* `FROM` forgery-robust; an inbound "email reply = governance verdict" is a **new inbound trust surface**. R3 gives the safe design and it slots into m-1, not around it: the conductor **mints a server-signed, one-time, per-`(decision_id, seat, choice)` token** when it mirrors the gate out; the reply carries the token back; **the token signature IS the m-1 channel-stamp brought inbound** (verify sig → audience → expiry → nonce-unused → seat-matches-expected). **POST/interstitial, never GET** (email scanners auto-click GET = silent auto-approve — a real foot-gun, R3/AWS-one-time-URL). **Separate the replay-nonce (one-shot) from the validity-window (long, for away-mode vacation).** Prior art: Outlook Actionable-Messages signed-JWT, Slack HMAC-over-raw-body (5-min window), AWS one-time approval URLs.
  - **→ m-1 COORD (DESIGN-phase):** the inbound verdict-token is an **m-1-adjacent identity primitive** (mint + verify is the TCB's job). *Does m-1's `mint_seat`/channel-stamp model extend to minting/verifying an inbound one-time verdict-token, or is this an m-6 construct over m-1-owned crypto?* This is a hard-to-reverse cross-domain decision = **grill material**. I flag it now; I do not pre-decide it.
- **Delegation (flag, likely DEFER):** R3's OOF→backup-approver pattern assumes multiple human approvers; our governance team is **solo-operator**, so delegation is likely out-of-scope for v3.0 — flagged as an operator-judgment item (§7), not designed here. If ever added, a delegation must be a **stamped, auditable relay** to a backup seat (m-1 graph), never silent substitution.
