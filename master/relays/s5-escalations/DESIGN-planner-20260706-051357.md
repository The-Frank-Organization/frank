### #1 (m-6 RULES) — the Step-1 known-A signal set: three content-derived sources, all already locked; `other`→A hardcoded
Under the §J2-A-as-config home (Q5's ruling), Step-1 known-A detection reads **three pick-independent, content-derived signals** — no new per-category predicate engine:

- **(S1) — the CQ-3 pure-judgment A-floor, computed from `(phase × record_kind)` — the PRIMARY "A-worthy when the pick is B" signal.** The A-floor table (canonical home `c4-cq-gateconfig/DESIGN-planner-20260702-015800`; NF-S8 binds there) maps `(phase × record_kind)` → a floor `gate_category`. Both inputs are header/content atoms present on the submission at the Q5 validate locus — **neither is the agent's pick.** If the floor category ∈ §J2 A-set and the effective pick/absorb is below it, the record **is A-worthy structurally**. This is the pure-content structural floor by design (it "fires from record class regardless of seat self-assessment" — the confused-agent guardrail). It is the main answer to the escalation's question.
- **(S2) — the referenced-gate-record's own `gate_category` (store lookup at the verdict path).** The pair found it detectable at submit.go:216-245. When a submission **handles/references a live gate** (a verdict relay, a resummon, a bounce-repair), that referenced gate's category is authoritative content; a B-pick on the handling submission **cannot de-classify a referenced live A-gate**. This is the direct code realization of my §2 "routed/absorbed as B ⇒ reject + raise" vector.
- **(S3) — the §J2 merge-split content predicate (`target-branch × protected-branch-set`).** The one fine-grained content predicate the locked design **already contains** — it computes `merge_to_protected` (A) from content even when the pick is `merge_feature_to_feature` (B). It ships in Step-1 because it already exists; the protected-branch-set is operator-config (§J2).
- **Fail-safe (unchanged):** `other`→A **hardcoded**, never config-editable-away.

**The detector predicate (Step-1):** compute the content-derived A-worthiness from `MAX(S1, S2, S3)`; if that ∈ §J2 A-set and the effective handling is below A (B pick/B absorb), **reject + raise to A + record the raised-to category** (never silently orchestrator-absorbed). This composes as one MAX-input raiser into m-2's monotonic model, at the Q5 locus, alongside the agent's own monotonic-RAISE pick.

**Config vs mechanism split:** the **config** is the A-floor table (S1) + the §J2 A/B map + the merge protected-branch-set (S3) — operator-tunable, under the §J2-A-as-config home. The **referenced-category lookup (S2) is mechanism**, not config (a store read at the verdict path, m-7-wired).

**Honest Step-1 residual [VP-W1 — stated, not overclaimed].** Per-category content predicates *beyond* (S1)'s `(phase × record_kind)` granularity and (S3)'s merge case are **NOT built in Step-1** — no per-category content schema exists yet (that is a Step-2+ carry). So a mis-pick that (a) the coarse `(phase × record_kind)` floor cannot distinguish within its cell, (b) is not the merge case, and (c) references no live A-gate, falls back to the **agent's monotonic-RAISE pick + the `other`→A fail-safe**. Step-1 therefore claims exactly `(S1)+(S2)+(S3)+other-fail-safe`, **not** "catches every content-mis-pick." I would rather s5 register that true boundary than a detector that reads stronger than the code.

### #2 (m-7 RULES the mechanics) — the m-6 consumer requirement that constrains it
m-7 owns whether the raise **rewrites the effective `gate_category` token** to the A member or **stamps a bool only**. m-6 does not rule that — but two things the m-6 surface **requires** of whichever m-7 chooses:
1. **The EFFECTIVE category that downstream bucket routing keys on must be the A member.** My bucket-A trigger keys on `gate_category ∈ §J2 A-set` (design §2). If the raise stamps only a nameless bool and leaves the B token, bucket routing would still read B and mis-route the raised record to bucket B — so either the token is rewritten to the A member, **or** the bucket router reads an explicit `gate_category_effective` field. m-6 needs the effective A category, by whatever field.
2. **The raised-TO A-category must be RECORDED (named), and the original agent pick preserved.** The ODB (§3) renders the A-lane with a **"raised to A as `<category>` because `<signal>`"** reason; the ③ audit needs the original B pick to detect the mis-pick pattern. A bare bool cannot render either. So m-6 needs the **raised-to category name** + the **original pick** both present.

**m-6's non-binding preference** (m-7's call): **rewrite the effective token to the A member AND preserve the original pick in a provenance field** best serves the surface — bucket routing "just works" on `gate_category ∈ A-set` with no special-case bool check, and the ODB/audit read the provenance for the "raised-because" render. But any m-7 realization satisfying (1)+(2) is acceptable to m-6. It must stay **atomic at the Q5 locus** and **byte-stamp the row's own enum token (`"yes"`, per the DEF-1 fix)** — I confirm DEF-1's byte fix is correct and note the ③ stamp writing `"true"` into the `["no","yes"]` enum is exactly the kind of self-bounce the direction-invariant must not introduce.
