## Ask 2 — Does `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, boundary marker_digest}` already prove exact resumed-context identity, or is an independent context witness required?

**Answer: an independent context witness IS warranted. The three members pin the durable LOG; they do NOT independently witness the ASSEMBLY of that log into the model-visible `input[]`.** Proof and the two-part reason, at my bytes:

### What the three members DO prove — PROVEN, not lexically asserted
The boundary `marker_digest` **transitively pins the entire durable valid prefix's bytes**, by chain construction:
- §1.3: `record_digest` = SHA-256 over the record's canonical bytes **including the envelope `prev_digest`** (self-excluding only `record_digest`); `prev_digest` = the immediately-preceding record's `record_digest` within the segment (seed for `segment_open`); cross-segment, `segment_open.prior_boundary_digest` = the predecessor's boundary `record_digest` (§1.7), and `segment_open`'s own `record_digest` covers it.
- §1.5: `marker_digest` ranges over `{seq, record_digest}` for the boundary round. The first boundary-round record's `record_digest` includes its `prev_digest`, which chains back through every prior record to genesis. **Therefore any divergence anywhere in the prefix changes a `prev_digest` → a `record_digest` → the boundary `marker_digest`.** Equal boundary `marker_digest` ⇒ the whole logged prefix is byte-identical. This is a derivation over the chain, not a lexical search.
- The logged prefix **includes the model-visible content**: `input_item` = "assembled `input[]` content" (§1.2 line 65), `compaction_event` = `{tier∈{evict,summarize}, template_id, template_version, affected_seq[], summary_item_index?}` (line 69), both **admit-eligible** (line 96). Tier-2 summary OUTPUT, once produced, enters `input[]` and is logged as an `input_item`.

So the durable, LOGGED model-visible context IS transitively pinned by the boundary `marker_digest`.

### Why that is NOT sufficient — the gap a context witness fills (your exact concern, confirmed)
**The three members witness the LOG. They do not witness the ASSEMBLY of the log into the `input[]` the model actually receives on resume.** Two independent facts at my bytes make this a real, not hypothetical, gap:
1. **§6 deliberately EXCLUDES assembled `input[]` content from `logical_surface_digest`** (line 389: "a reassembly touches assembled input content, which is **not a member**"). So *no existing digest* — not `marker_digest` (log), not `logical_surface_digest` (pre-lowering surface) — witnesses the assembled model-visible `input[]`. There is a genuine uncovered surface.
2. **Governance-Decay is exactly this class:** the model-visible context is subject to the governed 3-tier compaction (evict/summarize). `marker_digest` proves the logged records survived; it does **not** prove the resume ASSEMBLY reproduced the same `input[]` from them. A replay/re-compaction/assembly defect — or a divergence between "replay the logged summary output" and "re-run the summary" — would present the model a **different assembled context while `marker_digest` still matches**, because `marker_digest` digests the log, not the assembly-of-the-log. **So yes: two runs could share all three members and still present the model a different assembled context**, precisely the divergence you named.
   - The redundancy would hold **only** under a stronger invariant than my frozen design states: that resume is **pure verbatim replay** of the logged `input_item` records with **no re-assembly and no re-compaction**. My design asserts clean-path replay in prose (§3 line 336: content present ⇒ replayed, never reconstructed; only `content_lost`→`re_derive` re-reasons) but does **not** pin a byte-level "assembled `input[]` == logged `input_item` records" invariant at the assembly boundary. Absent that pinned invariant, the witness is not redundant.

### The recipe (since required) — producer/consumer split + observer-executable
- **`context_digest` = SHA-256 over the JCS of the assembled model-visible `input[]`** (the ordered `input_item` content — `role`, `item_index`, `content`, `source_tool_call_id?`) presented on the **resumed turn's first attempt**, in the exact m8.llm_request.v1 `input[]` order.
- **Producer:** m-9 — the resume assembly builds that `input[]`.
- **Observer-executable / consumer:** the **E3 observer captures the resumed turn's first-attempt wire request and hashes its `input[]`** — the *same* capture path already used for `logical_surface_digest` (§6), a *different scope* (the assembled content §6 excludes). This is what makes it an **independent** witness: it hashes what the model **actually received on the wire**, not m-9's own log, so it catches an assembly/replay bug that `marker_digest` (log) and the encoder cannot self-cancel. The fixture compares it to the frozen `xit-dur-1` expected assembled `input[]`.
- **No conflation:** three complementary digests — `marker_digest` (durable log survived), `logical_surface_digest` (pre-lowering surface, §6), `context_digest` (assembled model-visible input[], the §6-excluded content). Each witnesses a distinct surface.

### Scope carve-outs stated honestly (not a gap in the witness)
- **`reasoning_replay` (K6, §1.2 line 73)** is model-visible input but **never logged** (in-memory only) and is **deterministically absent on any resume** (§3 line 336). So it is a resume-vs-non-resume difference, **not** a two-resume divergence; a `context_digest` over the resumed `input[]` correctly reflects its (deterministic) absence. Witnessing the *pre-crash* in-memory context is impossible by K6 and is not what "resumed-context identity" can mean.
- **The `content_lost`→`re_derive` DEGRADED path** re-reasons non-deterministically (§3 lines 336/360); **no digest** (context or marker) is a meaningful durability oracle there. `xit-dur-1` is the **positive** resume, so it should exercise the clean-replay path only — the `context_digest` is meaningful exactly where the test lives.
