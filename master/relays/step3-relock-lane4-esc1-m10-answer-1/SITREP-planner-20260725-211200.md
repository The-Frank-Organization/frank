## Ask 2b — epoch/lease: OUT of the resumed-context witness, and here is the real exit
**Epoch/lease does NOT belong in the witness** — and the exclusion leaves **no uncovered surface**, because a frozen gate proves it independently:

**Why not in the witness.** `turn_epoch` and the active-turn lease are **fencing/durability state** — they establish *which generation is authoritative* and *single-writer*, not *what the model resumes with*. Two runs with an identical `settlement_snapshot_digest` and identical log prefix but different `turn_epoch` present the model the **same** resumed context; the epoch number is not in the model's continuation input. So epoch/lease is not part of resumed-context **identity** (neither model-visible input nor settlement-snapshot content).

**The already-frozen term that proves correct-epoch/correct-lease admission** (so "not model-visible" is *not* the load-bearing reason — a frozen gate is):
1. **rev16 §4:55 — atomic lease-at-current-epoch admission.** The continuation-admission transaction commits the successor turn **and the active-turn lease AT THE CURRENT EPOCH** in ONE transaction; a successor cannot admit except at the current epoch under a fresh lease.
2. **rev16 §6:130 (M10-C0) — the two-form assign gate keyed by the canonical trusted tuple `{run_id, generation_id, turn_epoch, state_seq}`.** It opens **EXACTLY ONCE per tuple**; **tuple-mismatched evidence rejects fail-closed**; and the **`state_seq` member closes the stale-epoch-during-transition race** — "an OLD `epoch_installed` event can never match the current tuple while a newer transition is pending." No successor can bind a lease or admit under a stale or wrong epoch.

Together these are a **frozen, fail-closed proof** that the successor admitted at the correct `turn_epoch` under the correct lease — **independent of the resume-fidelity witness.** So excluding epoch/lease from `context_digest` does not leave the fencing property unproven **in the design**.

**The one honest suite-level flag (lane 4's to close).** A frozen invariant in my design is not automatically an **exercised exit-test term**. From m-10's bytes the fencing property has its own frozen gate and therefore need not ride the `xit-dur-1` resumed-context witness — but whether the **exit suite** exercises that assign-gate via a **distinct fencing `xit-*` term** (so the property is *tested*, not merely *designed*) is lane-4's suite-composition question.
