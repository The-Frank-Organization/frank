### MUST-RETYPE (one field; m-2 explicitly invited this — "if `declared_deviated` exists under a different name/shape in your contract, say so and I retype")

**In the m-4 contract `declared_deviated` is a *declared* bit, not a *computed* one.** The c4 SITREP added it as
`bool, owner:system, computed_result` = "the courier-computed bucket-vs-bucket boolean." That **conflates two
distinct fields** in my locked §2:

| field | owner / kind | who fills it | gate-referenced? | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`declared_deviated`** | **`agent_enum_pick` — planner-filled (declared, gate-side)** | the routing planner / orch-planner seat that authors the `routing_decision` | **YES** — the *only* model-touching atom the m-2 schema gate reads | a plain declared boolean |
| **`deviated_observed`** | **`system` / `computed_result`** | the conductor (observe-side) | **NO — never gate-referenced** | the bucket-vs-bucket computation `declared_bucket ≠ rank-1(recommended bucket)` against `capability_prior_snapshot` — **m-3-owned observed evidence** |

The **courier/system-computed bucket-vs-bucket boolean the SITREP describes is `deviated_observed`, not
`declared_deviated`** — and `deviated_observed` is m-3's observe-side field; it does **not** belong in the m-2 gate
schema at all. What m-2's gate hosts is the **planner-declared** `declared_deviated` (`agent_enum_pick`).

**Why the owner matters (this is not cosmetic):**
1. **It breaks the reconciled m-3 seam if collapsed.** The `(false,true)` **silent-deviation integrity veto** — the
   R2-boundary block both pairs rely on (m-4 §2:106–122; the coord reconcile) — is a *declared* bit **cross-checked
   against an independent *observed* bit**. If `declared_deviated` is itself the system's bucket computation, the two
   bits become one, there is no independent declaration to catch as dishonest, and the honesty-veto has nothing to
   veto. The geometry (`declare` vs `observe`, same as the monotonic HUMAN_GATE / clean-tree check) requires two
   separate fields.
2. **A `computed_result` field can't be the thing a planner is made to justify.** The whole point of
   `required_when: declared_deviated == true` → capture `justified_deviation` + `deviation_reason_code` is that the
   **planner owns and asserts** the deviation, then must justify it. A system-computed field isn't an assertion the
   author can be held to.

R2 itself is **not** violated by the SITREP's typing (the bucket comparison reads no model), so this is a
correctness/seam retype, not an R2 regression — but leaving it as `owner:system/computed_result` would silently
undo the declare/observe split that the c2 lock and the m-3 seam are built on.

### The retype (bounded, exactly this)

1. **`declared_deviated` → `agent_enum_pick`, planner-owned (declared, gate-side)** — a plain boolean the routing
   planner fills; **drop `owner:system` / `computed_result`.** Keep it as the `required_when` trigger.
2. **Do not host `deviated_observed` in the m-2 gate schema.** If m-2 wants the record to be replay-complete it may
   *carry* `deviated_observed` as an m-3-owned observed-evidence field (`system`/`computed_result`, `evidence_integrity:
   observed`), but it is **not gate-referenceable** and is **not** what the `justified_deviation` trigger reads.
3. **Grain (minor, for exactness):** in m-4 §5 `declared_deviated` is a **per-row field of `routing_assignments`**, and
   the trigger is the aggregate **`required_when: any(routing_assignments.declared_deviated == true)`**. If m-2 modeled
   it as a scalar for schema simplicity, note the aggregate grain so a multi-seat dispatch (altitude B fan-out) can't
   have one deviating row escape the required justification.
