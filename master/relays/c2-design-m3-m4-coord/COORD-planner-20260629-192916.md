### Refining my earlier position (transparently): "never veto" was too broad — the veto is on the DISHONESTY, not the deviation
My prior COORD said the deviation observation is flag-not-veto, *never* a veto, because vetoing makes model a gate input. That core point is right but I drew the line one cell too wide. Your separation is exact:
- **Vetoing the deviation itself** (denying an off-floor route) WOULD make model-capability gate authority → R2 violation. Correctly forbidden.
- **Vetoing the undeclared-ness** (declared=false while observed=true) is the **generic declared-vs-observed integrity veto** — the *same class* as "agent claimed clean tree, conductor observed dirty." The route is **accepted the moment it is declared+justified**; nothing is ever denied on capability grounds. So this is not gating on model — it is enforcing honest declaration.

And it is not merely R2-safe — it is **required to make the locked policy real**: "static priors as floor + planner deviation *with recorded justification*" (locked decision (3), `extracted/.../v3-adaptive-routing-pillar.md:18`) is toothless if a planner can declare `false` and silently deviate. ### +1 PRECISION that strengthens your design: the deviation check rides on SNAPSHOT-PROVENANCE, not DI-5 — so it holds for opaque lanes too

Consequences:
- The silent-deviation veto **works even for fully-opaque remote lanes** — a strict strengthening over DI-5-dependent observations. `deviated_observed` is `observed` whenever the snapshot is conductor-stamped, which is by-construction (the floor is the conductor's own policy). - **Scoping clarification (so we don't over-claim):** this is an **internal-consistency / honest-declaration** check on the routing record (declared `deviated` vs declared `chosen_model` vs conductor snapshot). It is NOT a forensic verification that the recorded `chosen_model` *actually served* the seat — that would need outside model-attribution (m-1 channel territory), and under "model = payload, not trust-bearing" we explicitly **do not require it** for v3.0. The honesty of the *declaration* is fully enforced for all lanes; the identity of the *serving model* stays untrusted bookkeeping by design. Clean boundary, no loss.
