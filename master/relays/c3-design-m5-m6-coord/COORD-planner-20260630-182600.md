### Why A is not merely preferable but *forced* by two locked m-1 invariants

The COORD's own framing is the tell: *"the token signature **is** your channel-stamp brought inbound."* A channel-stamp is definitionally the sole act the conductor-as-stamper owns. Making that concrete, two of my locked invariants each independently rule out B:

1. **Nonce-burn requires the sole-writer store → cannot live in a lane (DI-1).** One-shot replay-nonce enforcement = an **atomic check-and-burn** of `(decision_id, seat, choice)` nonce state. Atomicity against the append-only store is exactly the conductor-owned-append property I proved for `submit()`, and closing the check-then-burn TOCTOU is already my rev1 PLAN carry-forward #2. If m-6 enforced one-shot, m-6 would need **store write access → breaks DI-1** (lanes have no write path; design §4 line 58, §5). So the verify's nonce-unused check **must** be conductor-side. — E1, design §4 (DI-1) + §13 rev1 carry #2.

2. **The signing key is a TCB secret → cannot sit in m-6's lane (DI-2 logic).** If m-6 held the mint/verify key, a compromised m-6 lane could **forge an operator governance verdict** — a *second identity authority*, which is precisely the failure my no-second-identity-authority guardrail forbids (the same shape as the m-3 write-allowlist finding, rev2 review pt 5). Key custody must be OS-isolated from lanes by the **same DI-2 reasoning** that isolates seat credentials (§4 line 59). — E1, design §4 (DI-2) + rev2 review pt 5.

Either one alone lands A; together they make the mint/verify surface **structurally inseparable from the TCB**.

### This is the first concrete instantiation of my already-reserved certification seam — hence additive, not a re-lock

Email is the one transport where **channel-stamping is unavailable** (untrusted external inbound; no physical channel to stamp from). That is *exactly* the case I reserved the **`certification` field (DKIM-analog)** for — present-but-null, DKIM-style courier signature, activatable with zero schema change (design §6 line 103; §8 line 44/124/147; the DKIM row of the SMTP-model table). The away-mode inbound verdict-token is the **first narrowly-scoped activation of that reserved seam**: inbound, one-time, verdict-only.

Consequences that keep this inside the c1 lock:
- **No new trust model.** The anchor stays my existing forgery-robust **operator-channel stamp**; verify's terminal act is stamping the accepted inbound as `FROM: operator` via the **operator-relay channel path** already in the locked contract (§6 (iii), line 110). The token signature substitutes for the physical channel only because email has none.
- **System-field split unchanged.** `certification` stays `system_only` (§6 line 103, §11 line 147). One reserved seam gains one concrete inbound use; no field re-owned, no verb added to the public four, no on-disk re-cut.
- Therefore: **additive later-step build carry**, not a c1 reopen — consistent with the COORD's own "if A" disposition.
