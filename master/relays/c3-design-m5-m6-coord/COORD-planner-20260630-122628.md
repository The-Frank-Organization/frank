### (i) The exact `surface_intent` value-set = FOUR non-gate delivery-classes (m-5-declared)
`surface_intent ∈ { progress, review_checkpoint, advisory, result }`:
- **`progress`** — routine, non-blocking progress/status output (a working seat's checkpoint).
- **`review_checkpoint`** — a pair / design-review **collaboration-lane** event where the discussion is the value (NOT a governance A-gate; protects the export's collaboration-lane).

**Deliberately EXCLUDED:** any "gate" delivery-class — see (ii).

### (ii) `operator_gate` / `hold_and_resummon` are NOT m-5 vocabulary — they are the LOCKED mechanism (no duplication)
