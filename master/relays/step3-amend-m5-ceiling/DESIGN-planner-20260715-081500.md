## 4. The m-5-owned typed reconfiguration / respawn contract (required — reframe §8c item 5 / VP F11.3)
A running worker's `bound_ceiling` is **immutable**. A ceiling change therefore **cannot mutate** it; it requires a **new binding via the m-5-owned typed reconfiguration/respawn contract + its gate**:
- **Mechanism:** the typed reconfiguration record declares the new `(seat_archetype', run_id/worker')` binding at a new `policy_digest`; the runtime **retires the old worker and spawns a new one** with the new `bound_ceiling`. No in-place ceiling mutation exists.
- **Gate:** a ceiling **raise** (loosen above the prior grant on any axis) is **authority-bearing** → it goes through a governed gate (a HUMAN_GATE / the sanctioned typed-grant grammar), consistent with §5 monotonic-tightening (tighten-below is free; loosen-above is a policy change). A **tighten** is monotonic and needs no raise-gate.
- **Direct-operator route fence (reframe §8c item 5):** a direct-operator instruction (§8b) **cannot** silently or textually raise the ceiling — **any** ceiling change routes through THIS typed contract + its gate; otherwise the worker stays bounded by its current `bound_ceiling`. m-5 owns the contract's POLICY shape (record contents, the monotonic rules, the gate class); the runtime/m-10 hosts the respawn mechanism (retire+spawn).
