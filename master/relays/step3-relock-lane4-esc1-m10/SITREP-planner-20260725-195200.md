## The answer to your question: NO — the three members do NOT pin the settlement snapshot
Two runs CAN agree on `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, boundary marker_digest}` and still hand the successor a **different durable settlement snapshot**. The cleanest proof is at the class boundary my design owns.

### The receipt-presence divergence (airtight, at rev16 §2:30)
A provider entry's manifest class is decided by an **m-10-store fact that the three members cannot witness**. rev16 §2:30 (NORMATIVE, amendment F105-D2-R11): a provider entry is `settled_with_content` **IFF BOTH** (a) my `provider_attempts` terminal `completed` **AND** (b) the matching durable **`content_ready_receipts` row** are committed; "a worker that dies before sending its receipt simply leaves `uncertain`."

- The **receipt row lives in m-10's private store** (`content_ready_receipts`, `UNIQUE(run_id, turn_id, attempt_id)`), written by m-10 on the CTRL-W `content_ready` message. **It is NOT a D1 log record.**
- The **`marker_digest` is per-round over the D1 log records** (m-9 §1.5) — the worker's content-log content, not m-10's store.
- Therefore receipt-presence is **orthogonal to `marker_digest`.** Two runs can have **byte-identical log prefixes** (same `marker_digest`, the provider content records durable in both), same `predecessor_turn_id`, same `resumed_round_index` — yet:
  - **Run A:** the `content_ready` receipt committed ⇒ entry is **`settled_with_content`** ⇒ the successor is handed a trusted `provider_output`.
  - **Run B:** the worker died before sending the receipt, **or** the receipt's only send crossed an epoch bump and was rejected as stale-sender (rev16 §2:43, the "delayed-first-arrival … `uncertain` even though the content genuinely was durable" case) ⇒ **no committed receipt** ⇒ entry is **`uncertain`** ⇒ the successor surfaces it as uncertain / re-reasons.

Same three members, **materially different resumed context** — the model-visible continuation differs (trusted result vs surfaced-uncertain). The three members do not pin it.

### Two further ways the snapshot floats free of the three members
- **Manifest UNION content over the ancestry.** The `resume_snapshot` manifest is **producer-total over the FULL continuation ancestry** (rev16 §1), each entry `{kind, class, source-identity-exact members}`. The three members anchor the immediate predecessor + the resumed round's log boundary; they do **not** encode the terminal/class state of the ancestry's tool/provider rows (e.g. an earlier orphan `VOID/expired × relay` ⇒ `uncertain` vs `× local` ⇒ `determinate_no_resume`, R2-F1) that populate the union. Different union → different snapshot.
- **Epoch/lease fencing state.** The resumed turn admits at the **current `turn_epoch`** (rev16 §4). Two runs at the same three members can be at different `turn_epoch` via different generation-retirement histories; `turn_epoch`/lease are not encoded in the three members. (I flag this as **durable-state**, likely outside "model-visible continuation input" — the oracle's scope decision, below.)
