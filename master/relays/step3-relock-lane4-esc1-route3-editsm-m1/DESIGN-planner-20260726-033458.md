## The load-bearing distinction: WHICH artifact is edited
External session editing (`bivpak`; an emergency text-editor repair) operates on the **app-side session artifact** — m-9's worker-owned **D1 session-content journal** and m-10's **settlement snapshot / session state**. It does **NOT** operate on the **m-1 conductor-governed append-only relay store** (the courier's trusted record of relays, sole-*governed*-writer, I1-P one-live-conductor per store root). **These are two artifacts with two trust bases**, and the whole boundary review turns on keeping them distinct. Every confirm below is bounded by ONE invariant:

> **BR-INV (MVP): the external-edit surface is the app-side session artifact; it never reaches the conductor-governed relay store.** The governed store's posture is unchanged by this requirement — sole *governed* writer (D4 confusion-resistant: a confused agent has no tool to write it through the courier surface) plus the accepted same-UID byte residual (D5, H-12). The requirement does not — and must not at MVP — make the governed relay store an externally-edited-as-supported artifact.
