## Q1 — Is §7's `log_prefix_digest` my §1.5 `marker_digest`? — NOT A STATED IDENTITY, and the scope is not unique

**First, the negative, byte-verified:** `log_prefix_digest` appears **zero** times in my frozen delta (`grep` for `log_prefix_digest`/`prefix_digest`/`resume_prefix` ⇒ none). My delta never names this field, so there is **no stated identity** between it and anything I define. Treating it as my `marker_digest` would be building the frozen oracle on inference — exactly what you and the lane-4 planner refused to do, correctly.

**What I DO define — `marker_digest` (§1.5, line 122), exact recipe:**
> `marker_digest` = **SHA-256 over the JCS of the ordered array of `{seq, record_digest}` for every record in the INCLUSIVE interval `[first_seq, last_seq]`** of ONE honoured `round_marker` (schema `{round_index, first_seq, last_seq, marker_digest}`, §1.5 line 70). `record_digest` is §1.3's per-record digest over the record's canonical bytes **including the envelope `segment_id`**. Honour requires the interval be contiguous/exactly-bounded, well-linked, and that no admit-eligible record outside `[first_seq,last_seq]` carries the same round key.

**The decisive fact: `marker_digest` is PER-ROUND, not per-prefix.** It ranges over a single round's interval. `log_prefix_digest` — by name — reads as a digest over the log's *prefix*. Those are not the same object, and the amendment's vector `{predecessor_turn_id, resumed_round_index, log_prefix_digest, context_digest}` admits **three non-equivalent readings**, which is why I cannot answer "yes":
- **(a) the `marker_digest` of the resumed round** (the `round_marker` for `resumed_round_index`) — a per-round value, fully defined by §1.5;
- **(b) a value identifying the durable VALID PREFIX at the resume boundary** — in my design the valid prefix truncates to the **last honoured `round_marker`** (§1.8 line 243; the `boundary_record`, §1.7 line 178), and §3 (line 303) already binds the content-ready receipt to a **"valid-prefix/marker digest"** — i.e. the **boundary round_marker's `marker_digest`**, which because every record chains via `prev_digest`/`record_digest` and a moved/altered prefix fails closed, is **prefix-DETERMINING**. So the boundary marker's `marker_digest` is the m-9 quantity that *identifies* the valid prefix — but it is a per-round marker used as a prefix identity, not a distinct recipe;
- **(c) a single digest ranging over ALL records of the whole (possibly multi-round) valid prefix** — **my delta defines NO such digest.** I define per-round `marker_digest`s only; there is no one-value digest over the entire multi-round prefix.

**So my answer to Q1:** it is **not** a settled identity. IF the amendment's authoring intent is (a) or (b), then §1.5 supplies the exact recipe (inclusive `[first_seq, last_seq]`, over the honoured `round_marker`'s own interval; for (b), the boundary = last honoured marker per §1.7/§1.8) and I confirm THAT recipe — but **which round/interval it ranges over is the amendment's to pin, not mine to infer.** IF the intent is (c) — a whole-prefix digest distinct from any single marker — **no frozen recipe exists**, and authoring one is amendment-shaped. My non-binding lean, since you asked what to do with the answer: (b) is the most coherent reading of "the positive resume must reproduce ... `log_prefix_digest`" — the resume reproduces the durable prefix, and the boundary marker's `marker_digest` is precisely how my design already identifies a valid prefix (§3 line 303). But a lean is not a stated identity; **the field's meaning must be ratified (Master+VP+operator), because picking (a)/(b)/(c) selects different frozen values from the same run.**
