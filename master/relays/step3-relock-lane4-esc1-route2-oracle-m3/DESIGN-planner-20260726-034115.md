## Item 6 (shape) + the E3 predicate — `valid_prefix_matches_frozen_expected` v1

## The frozen-interval proof — my side (LANE4-ESC1-VP4-F1)
The comparison binds the frozen interval, not the append tail, because **`boundary_seq` is derived from FROZEN material, never from where the file ends at read time**:
- The FROZEN expected artifact has a definite extent — its last record's `seq` is `boundary_seq` (a closed function of frozen fixture bytes).
- The durable content-ready receipt's **`seq_hwm`** independently records the committed-end of the valid prefix at the crash boundary (my Route-5 ruling keeps it present for exactly this). The two must agree; the leg uses the frozen `boundary_seq` as the authoritative stop.
- The actual-side locator reads `[first_seq … boundary_seq]` and **excludes every record with `seq > boundary_seq`** — i.e. the entire post-resume append tail is outside the compared interval **by construction**. So a file that has legitimately grown past the resume point neither fails spuriously nor silently re-scopes the comparison to the reader's stop position; the stop is frozen-derived.
- **The joint seam:** m-9 (item 1) owns how "the valid prefix" and its end are determined at extraction; I bind my locator's `boundary_seq` to that same frozen end. Neither half is sound alone — m-9 defines the boundary; I prove the comparison honours it.

## Item 5 (mismatch diagnostic) — designed, not assumed
On `fail`, the leg reports the **diff of the two held artifacts** (frozen expected prefix vs bounded actual prefix). **Produced by the HARNESS, at predicate-evaluation time**, because the harness holds both sides (condition (iv) + the observer-read actual). The diagnostic = the first differing record position + both records' canonical bytes. This restores the per-position granularity the operator noted comes free from holding both files — **as a post-`fail` diagnostic over held content, NOT as a stored comparison term.** It is not the ordered digest list in another costume: no per-record digests are computed or persisted as the leg's evidence; the diff is a failure read-out over content already in hand, which is precisely the operator's stated rationale.
