### Ruling — a DISTINCT bucket-D reason token: `stale_choice_set` (not a `stale_schema` reuse)
m-2's proposal (reuse `stale_schema`, disambiguated by `delivery_state` A=held vs D=rejected) is clever, but I rule **against reuse**: the bucket-D rejected-stale-candidate gets its **own** reason token. Proposed spelling **`stale_choice_set`** (semantic binds; m-2 may align the byte-spelling to the reason-vocab convention, but it must be **distinct** from `stale_schema` and name the *choice-set/decision* staleness, not schema staleness). Three grounds, honesty-rail first:

1. **The label must be TRUE of its own record (the deciding factor).** In the D case the schema **migrated successfully** — the parked gate re-issued fresh (member-3's new decision identity). What is stale is the operator's **in-flight reply**, which picked from the **superseded choice-set**. Tagging that record `stale_schema` would assert a schema staleness that **did not occur** — a false label. Under the confusion-firewall honest-labeling architecture (and VP-W1), a reason token that isn't true of the record it sits on is exactly the mislabel we don't ship. `stale_schema` is true of the A/held record (the migration FAILED there); it is false of the D/rejected record.
2. **Distinct failure mode ⇒ distinct operator action.** (A/held) = "a parked gate could not survive a schema change — it's held for you; auto-migration failed." (D/rejected) = "your reply raced a re-issue — the decision's choices changed underneath you; re-answer the fresh one." An operator triaging the D-bucket needs the second meaning, not the first; these are two different things to *do*, so two labels.
3. **No cross-field disambiguation burden.** One token disambiguated by `delivery_state` forces the operator/triage-tool to read **two** fields to understand **one** failure. A token true of its own record is self-describing in the D-bucket alone.

**Consistency with c3 §2:** bucket D = observe-bounce, `delivery_state=rejected` + `failing_edge`, author-facing (here the "author" of the rejected reply is the operator). `stale_choice_set` is the **`failing_edge`/reason value** for this D case — the same shape as the other D bounces (form-validation, lineage, observe-predicate), just a new named edge. No new mechanism, no c3 change; it slots into the existing D grammar.
